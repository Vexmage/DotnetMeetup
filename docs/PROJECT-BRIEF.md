# EugDotNet Project Brief

## Mission and identity

EugDotNet is an independent technology publication, engineering laboratory, professional directory, and community resource rooted in Eugene, Oregon, and the Pacific Northwest. It serves developers and other technically curious readers in the region and beyond through practical engineering writing, open experimentation, and regional professional and community information.

## History

EugDotNet began as a local .NET meetup. The existing DotnetMeetup application is the starting point for the publication. Its event records, presentations, attribution, and other historical material document genuine community activity and remain part of the project.

## Objectives and scope

- Publish independent technical articles, reporting, tutorials, and interviews through the Magazine.
- Present software projects, experiments, and demonstrations through the Lab.
- Support voluntary regional professional discovery through the Directory.
- Preserve meetup history and make regional events and resources available through Community.
- Establish an accessible editorial experience across Home, Magazine, Lab, Directory, Community, and About, with unfinished sections eligible to be omitted from public launch navigation.

The project evolves incrementally from the existing application. The [implementation plan](IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md) defines the release sequence and scope.
